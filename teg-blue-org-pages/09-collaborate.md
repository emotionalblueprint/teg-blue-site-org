# Collaborate

We welcome researchers who want to work with TEG-Blue's theoretical architecture.

This is not a request to validate everything. It is an invitation to pick a lane, test something specific, and help the system get stronger through honest engagement.

---

## What collaboration can look like

**Review:** Stress-test the theoretical mapping. Identify errors, missing foundations, oversimplified connections, or claims that need revision.

**Replication:** Attempt independent replication of the four-mode classification using different datasets, populations, or coding schemes.

**Co-authoring:** Collaborate on new validation studies, psychometric work, or cross-cultural testing with clear authorship agreements from the start.

**Advisory:** Offer expertise in a specific domain (neuroscience, psychometrics, NLP, clinical practice, AI safety) to help shape study design or methodology.

**Tool validation:** Help evaluate whether the 16 Emotional Tools are reliable, valid, non-harmful, and clinically usable.

---

## Authorship and attribution principles

- Authorship follows established academic norms (contribution-based, agreed in advance)
- Contributors are acknowledged on the site
- Significant contributions can receive per-section or per-study attribution
- Independent work that builds on TEG-Blue should cite the framework as the originating architecture

---

## Intellectual property boundaries

- The TEG-Blue architecture, framework names, and visual system are the intellectual property of the TEG-Blue Research Consortium
- All published research and data are licensed under CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0
- Collaboration agreements are discussed before work begins, not after
- We aim for open science by default, with clear exceptions when needed

## How to start

Send a short message with:
- Your background
- Which lane you want to contribute to (measurement, validation, theoretical review, AI alignment, tool evaluation)
- What you would test or critique first

**research@teg-blue.org**

## Current priorities

We are actively looking for collaborators in these areas:

1. **Independent replication** of the four-mode classification in natural language
2. **Psychometric validation** of the Emotional Tools (16 gradient scales)
3. **Cross-cultural and cross-linguistic** testing
4. **AI safety evaluation** — schema design, risk analysis, misuse prevention
5. **Theoretical review** — literature alignment, falsifiable predictions, missing foundations
