### Validation Study: Four-Mode Gradient in Natural Language
*Anna Paretas-Artacho, 2026*
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18428907

A computational analysis of 10,000+ natural conflict narratives (Reddit AITA posts) testing whether the Four-Mode Gradient can reliably detect emotional regulatory states in unstructured text.

**Key findings:**
- All four regulatory modes were successfully detected using polyvagal markers, contempt markers, and moral disengagement markers
- 33.8% of individuals escalated toward Control/Domination when challenged
- 22.2% de-escalated toward Connection
- De-escalators showed 78% higher rates of complexity markers than escalators
- Mode classifications correlated with independent community moral judgments
- 74% inter-rater reliability achieved

**Status:** Published. Computational analysis with preliminary evidence. Needs independent replication.

**Limitations:** Single dataset (Reddit AITA). Computational classification, not human clinical coding. English-language only. Cross-context and cross-cultural generalization not yet tested.

## What is still needed

**Independent replication:** The four-mode classification has been tested once. It needs independent researchers to attempt replication using different datasets, coding schemes, and populations.

**Psychometric validation:** The Emotional Tools (16 gradient scales) have been designed but not yet subjected to formal psychometric validation — factor analysis, reliability testing, convergent/discriminant validity.

**Cross-cultural testing:** All current evidence comes from English-language, Western-context data. Cross-cultural and cross-linguistic replication is a priority.

**Pre-registration:** Future studies will be pre-registered. We welcome collaborators who can help design and register formal validation protocols.
