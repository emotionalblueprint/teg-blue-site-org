# Gradient Modes Science Grounding

Draft status: working research notes for `.org`. Do not publish until source choices, citations, and claim language are reviewed.

Core positioning: TEG-Blue is a map: an integrative architecture that synthesises scientific research into a visual framework for reading emotional, nervous-system, relational, and repair patterns.

Purpose: build a mode-by-mode grounding map for the Gradient. The goal is not to claim that one established theory proves the whole Gradient. The goal is to show how the TEG-Blue architecture synthesises established research fields into a visual map of autonomic state, perception, cognition, body activation, tempo, emotion, empathy, action, repair, and chronic organization.

Working assumption: "14 modes" means the seven acute formations plus the seven chronic configurations. Adjust this if Anna means a different 14-mode set.

## Claim Discipline

Use this posture throughout:

> TEG-Blue is not claiming that established science has already validated the whole Gradient as a clinical model. It is showing that each described dimension of the Gradient is grounded in existing research on nervous-system state, emotion, perception, body activation, relational access, and repair.

Short version:

> TEG-Blue is a map: an integrative architecture that synthesises scientific research into a visual framework for reading emotional, nervous-system, relational, and repair patterns.

Each mode entry should separate:

- What TEG-Blue describes.
- Which part of the description is being grounded.
- Which research area or source supports that part.
- What TEG-Blue takes from the source.
- What TEG-Blue does not claim.

## Terminology Notes

- Use **mode** when referring to how the pattern feels or presents experientially.
- Use **state** when referring to the nervous-system state.
- Use **configuration** for the expression of mode + state: how the nervous system has configured around a precise need.
- Keep **formation** as an internal Deep Engine term. Formations describe how the body organizes around actual and present conditions.
- Use **A↔B** in public-facing terminology. Use **A<->B** only for ASCII-safe technical contexts: filenames, URLs/slugs, code keys, plain-text fallbacks, and systems that may not handle the arrow well.
- Best pattern in docs: **A↔B Acute - Safety Checking**. First mention: **A↔B** (ASCII fallback: **A<->B**). Then use **A↔B** everywhere in public-facing writing.
- Keep **fawn** in public wording for Protection / Defence. Ground it carefully as appeasement / affiliation-under-threat rather than as a cleanly established autonomic pathway.
- Use **Polyvagal theory** in public copy as visible but modest support: present enough that readers recognize the lineage around social engagement, mobilisation, and shutdown, but not so much that TEG-Blue reads as a Polyvagal derivative. Treat it as one grounding layer and language bridge alongside broader autonomic neuroscience, stress physiology, affective neuroscience, attachment, trauma, and repair research.

## Mode Grounding Snapshot Index

This is the scan layer: the compact science-center list for each mode before the fuller source trace.

How this helps:

- It shows the integrative contribution without making the homepage carry a heavy bibliography.
- It lets each mode say, at a glance, which established research areas it is synthesising.
- It gives future public copy a source-disciplined route: mode -> grounded dimensions -> specific sources -> claim boundary.
- It helps avoid the wrong impression that TEG-Blue is inventing biology; the contribution is the visual architecture and pattern language that connects already studied mechanisms.
- It can feed a future `/scientific-foundations` or mode-grounding page, but the homepage should only use a very small summary of this layer.

Public-use rule:

- Use the index to write short integrative statements.
- Do not publish the full bullet lists on the homepage.
- Keep detailed source links in the scientific-grounding layer or a claim-audit bibliography.

Possible homepage distillation:

> Each mode is read across recurring grounded dimensions: autonomic state, perception, cognition, body activation, emotion, relational access, action, chronic organization, and repair. The science exists in established fields; TEG-Blue makes the relationships visible as one visual map.

### Acute

For Baseline:

- homeostasis / allostasis
- parasympathetic rest-and-digest physiology
- HRV / vagal regulation
- interoception and body awareness
- open cognition under lower threat load
- quiet emotion as “no dominant problem signal”
- recovery, repair, and unhurried time

For A Acute - Connection / Belonging, the snapshot now centers on:

- social safety, attachment, and co-regulation
- parasympathetic / ventral-vagal social engagement
- positive emotion broadening
- social perception: face, tone, timing, context, shared meaning
- flexible cognition under lower threat load
- empathy / mentalizing / the other as real and separate
- open tempo and low urgency
- rupture-repair and reconnection

For A↔B Acute - Safety Checking, the snapshot centers on:

- relational uncertainty and safety appraisal
- attachment-system activation: proximity, availability, rupture, and repair checking
- parasympathetic-to-sympathetic transition / early readiness
- neuroception and social threat detection
- attention to ambiguous social cues: tone, timing, distance, attention, mismatch
- anticipatory anxiety and uncertainty intolerance
- slight quickening, present-moment tightening, and repair urgency
- sensitive emotion: unease, shame, sadness, fear of rupture
- heightened social reading / mentalizing while the other is still reachable
- repair still within reach through clarification, reassurance, responsiveness, or boundary update

For B Acute - Protection / Defence, it centers on:

- sympathetic stress physiology
- fight-flight defense response
- threat appraisal / neuroception
- attentional narrowing and threat-salience
- stress effects on prefrontal cognition
- hyperarousal, urgency, rush, and time compression
- fear, anxiety, vigilance, boundary emotion
- resource diversion away from repair
- empathy and repair becoming secondary
- fawn framed carefully as appeasement / affiliation-under-threat

For C Acute - Strategic Management, it centers on:

- persistent threat and anticipatory stress
- sympathetic mobilisation with inhibitory control / vagal brake
- threat prediction, sequencing, containment, and control of variables
- cognitive control, executive monitoring, inhibition, and planning under load
- worry / perseverative cognition as repeated activation of stress representation
- emotion regulation and suppression: anxiety and tension held under management
- narrowed management field: softer cues filtered if they do not help the model
- pressured future-orientation and fast tempo
- reserve drawdown, allostatic cost, and reduced recovery
- the other read through prediction and management rather than open relational contact
- repair delayed because admitting impact can feel like destabilizing control

For D Acute - Power Mobilisation, it centers on:

- life-threat mobilisation and survival-level defensive output
- sympathetic activation and maximum action readiness
- threat imminence: danger close, blocked, or inescapable
- defensive survival circuits and active defense
- tunnel field: power, force, outcome, and blocked threat dominate perception
- survival-only cognition and reduced reflective range
- rage / anger as approach- and action-ready emotion
- maximum pressure and collapse of time to the survival instant
- empathy, impact, and another person's separate reality becoming hard to register
- repair out of reach until force output stands down and enough safety returns
- mechanism-effect language: state-shaped capacity without excusing harm or proving motive

For Z Acute - Shutdown, it centers on:

- capacity exceeded and conservation response
- shutdown as fallback outside the active X-D gradient
- freeze, tonic immobility, collapsed immobility, and passive defense
- parasympathetic / dorsal-vagal language as a careful Polyvagal bridge
- hypoarousal, dissociation, numbness, fog, blankness, and reduced contact
- withdrawn perception and reduced usable awareness
- slowed body output, stillness, and conservation
- frozen tempo: time stops, drops out, or becomes hard to sequence
- muted emotion: signals flatten, fade, or go offline
- relational withdrawal: attunement and empathy become hard to access
- repair blocked until protected re-engagement comes first

### Chronic

For X Chronic - Chronic Elevated Baseline, it centers on:

- allostasis and allostatic load
- chronic stress and raised activation floor
- stress-system recalibration rather than a clean return to baseline
- autonomic regulation, HRV, and reduced vagal flexibility
- HPA-axis / cortisol dysregulation at rest, handled carefully rather than as one fixed biomarker
- recovery, sleep, digestion, repair, and immune maintenance being under-resourced
- stress effects on prefrontal cognition and reflective range
- interoception and self-read becoming thinner or noisier against background activation
- background affect: unfinished signals, pressure, tension, irritability, or need under the surface
- lightly guarded care and self/other differentiation because the system is never fully at rest
- incomplete return: relief can lower pressure without fully resetting the raised floor

For A Chronic - No Safety Access / Approximated Bonding-Belonging, it centers on:

- attachment theory: secure base, proximity seeking, internal working models, and affect regulation
- social baseline theory and social buffering: close others can reduce threat load and metabolic effort
- co-regulation and interpersonal emotion regulation
- Polyvagal social-engagement language as a modest bridge, not the whole proof
- developmental safety learning: safety has to become internally available, not only externally supplied
- reduced inward read: body-signal and self-tracking can be replaced by narrative, pleasing, performance, or contact-maintenance
- social field partly narrowed: contact is scanned for loss, demand, rupture, mismatch, or rejection
- guarded or compensatory contact: approach may be sincere and needed while still organized around getting safety to land
- lightly reduced self/other differentiation: closeness can be preserved while separateness is harder to feel
- relief without completion: closeness may soothe pressure without building settled internal safety access

For A↔B Chronic - Chronic Safety Checking, it centers on:

- attachment-system activation and anxious proximity monitoring
- uncertainty and anticipation in anxiety
- rejection sensitivity and social-threat vigilance
- attentional bias toward threat, mismatch, and ambiguous social cues
- parasympathetic-to-sympathetic transition held as a chronic edge
- brake not fully re-engaging: readiness stays braced at the safety boundary
- rumination / perseverative cognition around unresolved rupture
- time looping around the same safety question
- bonding and risk signals fused: care, unease, shame, sadness, fear of rupture
- self-tracking caught on “am I still okay here?”
- the other read for mismatch or possible threat rather than freely
- repair/reassurance reducing pressure without closing the loop

For B Chronic - Chronic Protection / Defence, it centers on:

- chronic sympathetic mobilisation and defensive readiness
- allostatic load: stress systems stay costly when activation does not complete
- persistent current-threat models: the present is read through an unresolved threat template
- threat attention and attentional bias toward danger cues
- stress effects on prefrontal flexibility, reflective range, and updating
- hyperarousal, vigilance, bracing, avoidance, pursuit, resistance, and boundary defence
- fight, flight, freeze, and fawn, with fawn grounded as appeasement / affiliation-under-threat
- Connection-Defence Fusion: connection-preservation and organism-protection signals get tangled
- care filtered through defence; the other read through threat or boundary risk
- constant urgency: time feels behind, compressed, or locked in anticipation
- maintenance and repair under-resourced while protection remains primary
- repair requiring discharge, boundary action, distance, reliable safety, or actual threat resolution

For C Chronic - Chronic Strategy & Management, it centers on:

- persistent threat and sustained anticipatory stress
- perseverative cognition: worry, rumination, and repeated threat modelling
- cognitive control, inhibition, planning, monitoring, and control of variables
- sympathetic activation with inhibitory control / vagal brake as functional language
- emotion regulation and suppression: pressure contained through management
- narrative and coherence as regulation routes that may not complete body-level return
- predictive processing and threat-model confirmation: scanning for what fits the model
- distorted emotional signals: source, timing, scale, target, responsibility, or response requirement can mismatch
- reserve continuously drawn down while the composed surface hides physiological cost
- self-read replaced by the model: cognitive override stands in for feeling the state
- the other read through the management model: selective, instrumental, or controlled
- repair delayed because loosening the model can feel like losing the regulation route

For D Chronic - Chronic Power & Dominance, it centers on:

- repeated maximum-protection organisation and near-maximum output held
- sympathetic mobilisation and action readiness staying expensive and available
- threat imminence and survival-relevant certainty
- social power, dominance, approach motivation, and reduced inhibition
- coercive control: force, threat, surveillance, compliance, isolation, or control over another's options
- aggression research: cognition, affect, arousal, scripts, and social-interaction routes into harm
- moral disengagement: harm can be justified, minimized, displaced, or blamed on the target
- dehumanization / objectification: the other read as obstacle, resource, target, or threat
- empathy-disabled regulation: the other's distress does not update the route
- self-other differentiation collapse: separate subjectivity is unavailable or overridden
- relief escalating instead of repair: control can feel like settling while adding load
- effect-over-time filter: if empathy, accountability, reality, autonomy, and repair stay absent, the response moves toward boundary, protection, and accountability

For Z Chronic - Persistent Shutdown / Collapse, it centers on:

- capacity staying exceeded over time
- chronic conservation, passive defense, tonic immobility, collapsed immobility, and dissociative shutdown
- parasympathetic / dorsal-vagal language as a modest Polyvagal bridge, held alongside broader defense-cascade and autonomic neuroscience
- hypoarousal, affective numbing, depersonalization, derealization, and dissociation
- interoceptive collapse: self-read faint, fragmented, or numb
- withdrawn perception: the field narrows by disappearing, not by rushing forward
- foggy, blank, slowed, or offline cognition
- action unavailable: withdrawal, stillness, dissociation, or collapse as the default answer
- frozen, timeless, or hard-to-move-forward tempo
- relational attunement offline or distant; the other is not fully taken in
- collapse conserving demand rather than repairing the pattern
- protected re-engagement before active regulation, contact, repair, or accountability can run

## X Acute - Baseline / Physiological Baseline

### TEG-Blue Description

Baseline / Physiological Baseline describes safe and at rest. The system is not organized around threat, pursuit, repair, or social risk. The perceptual field is wide and low-pressure: body signals, context, other people, and available options can all stay in view without the system needing to sort the field for danger. Emotion is quiet because there is no dominant problem signal. Recovery, digestion, repair, reflection, and unhurried time are available.

Source trace in current `.org` data: Formation X / Physiological Baseline / Safe & Rest, Rest & Digest.

### Grounding Snapshot

Grounded around:

- Homeostasis / allostasis.
- Parasympathetic rest-and-digest physiology.
- HRV / vagal regulation.
- Interoception and body awareness.
- Open cognition under lower threat load.
- Quiet emotion as no dominant problem signal.
- Recovery, repair, and unhurried time.

## B Chronic - Chronic Protection / Defence

### TEG-Blue Description

B Chronic describes defence as the resting state. Sympathetic mobilisation stays held rather than discharged, so the body remains organized around protection before the present moment has fully updated. The field stays narrowed around threat cues. Defensive readiness stays available through bracing, avoidance, pursuit, resistance, boundary defence, freeze, or fawn / appeasement. Repair is difficult because mobilisation stays held; the body needs discharge, boundary action, distance, or threat resolution before repair can fully land.

## Z Chronic - Persistent Shutdown / Collapse

### TEG-Blue Description

Z Chronic describes shutdown as a persistent operating configuration. Capacity stays exceeded, so conservation, withdrawal, reduced output, numbness, fog, or collapse become difficult to leave. The field withdraws; less is available. Thinking can become blank, slowed, foggy, or hard to organise. Self-read is faint, fragmented, or numb. Relational attunement is offline or distant. Action is unavailable: withdrawal, stillness, dissociation, or collapse becomes the available answer. Collapse reduces demand, but it does not return capacity. The first need is protected re-engagement before active regulation, repair, accountability, or mutual contact can run.
