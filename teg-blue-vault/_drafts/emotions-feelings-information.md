# Emotions, Feelings, and Information

Draft status: working notes for a future `.org` page. Do not publish until the page shape, URL, and language have been reviewed.

## Short Intro

Emotions are embodied information signals. They tell the organism that something has become biologically or relationally relevant: connection, loss, threat, boundary, value, possibility, overload, or repair. A feeling is real as experience, but the formation holding it changes what becomes easy to feel, name, interpret, and update.
